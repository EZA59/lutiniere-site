Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Version française

In short: Lutinière collects no data. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything the app does stays on your iPad.

1. Who publishes the app?

Lutinière is published by EZA (“we”). Contact: eza@protector-bot.com.

2. Data used by the app

To work, Lutinière stores a few things only on your device:

  • Your forest and progress: trees, elves, buildings, coins, quests, achievements, discovered secrets.
  • Your focus session history: duration, chosen subject, date.
  • Your settings, and the first name you may choose to enter (optional), shown only inside the app.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is included in your iPad backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own Apple settings.

3. Screen Time (app blocking)

During a session, Lutinière blocks the apps you did not allow, using Apple’s Screen Time tools (Family Controls). You pick the allowed apps yourself in Apple’s picker. The app only receives anonymous tokens from Apple: we cannot know which apps you use or for how long. These tokens stay on the device.

4. Other device features

  • Notifications are scheduled locally on the iPad. No server is involved.
  • Motion: while the app is open, it detects when you shake the iPad to make the forest tremble. This is neither stored nor transmitted.
  • Lock screen (Live Activity): the remaining time of your session is shown on the lock screen and computed on the device.
  • iCloud sync: if you use Lutinière on several devices with the same Apple ID, your forest is copied to your own iCloud (Apple’s private storage) so it is the same everywhere. We have no access to it. You can turn sync off in Settings > your name > iCloud.

5. What we don’t do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data: we have none.
  • The app never connects to our servers: we don’t have any.

6. Children

Lutinière is suitable for all ages. Since the app collects no data, no information about children is collected.

7. Deleting your data

Delete the app from your iPad and all of its data is erased. You may also delete older iPad backups if you want no copy to remain.

8. Your rights

As we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or erase. You can still write to us at the address above with any question.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
